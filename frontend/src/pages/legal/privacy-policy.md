# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 1, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 1, 2026

> **Template notice:** This document is an educational-project template for the Smart Campus Resource Sharing portal and application. Before public deployment, the college or organisation should have it reviewed and adapted by qualified legal and privacy professionals, including for applicable Indian law and institutional rules.

## 1. Introduction

Smart Campus Resource Sharing (the **“Platform”**, **“we”**, **“us”**, or **“our”**) is a college-campus portal and application that helps authorised students, faculty, library staff, lab staff, and administrators find, share, reserve, issue, return, and manage academic resources. These resources may include books, notes, syllabi, study materials, previous-year question papers, blueprint materials, classrooms, and lab equipment.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the Platform processes, why it processes it, who can access it, how long it may be retained, and the choices available to users. It applies to the Platform’s website, mobile application, dashboards, notifications, and related services.

The institution operating the Platform is the primary organisation responsible for deciding why and how institutional user information is processed. References to “Institution” mean the college, university, or educational organisation that operates or sponsors the Platform.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect only information that is reasonably necessary to operate, secure, administer, and improve the Platform.

### 2.1 Account and identity information

When an account is created or used, we may process:

- Name
- Institutional email address
- Student ID, staff ID, or admin ID
- Encrypted or hashed account-password credentials
- Assigned role, such as Student, Faculty, Library Staff, Lab Staff, or Admin
- Account status, login timestamps, password-reset records, and security-related activity

### 2.2 Academic and profile information

Depending on the user’s role, the Platform may process:

- Department, course or programme, class, section, year, batch, designation, year joined, and subjects taught
- A profile photograph, if the user chooses to upload one
- Classroom memberships and faculty classroom/subject assignments
- User-provided profile fields and communications submitted through the Platform

Some fields, including department, role, student/staff ID, and assigned institutional details, may be controlled or locked by an administrator to maintain accurate records.

### 2.3 Resource and content information

We process content that users or authorised staff add to the Platform, including:

- Resource titles, descriptions, categories, subject tags, authorship/owner information, and upload dates
- Notes, syllabi, study materials, blueprints, question papers, and other uploaded documents
- Book and equipment inventory information, availability counts, and resource locations or sections
- File metadata needed to store, display, search, moderate, and deliver uploaded files

Users should not upload another person’s personal data, confidential institutional material, copyrighted material without permission, or sensitive information unless authorised by the Institution and legally permitted.

### 2.4 Booking, issue, return, and history information

To manage campus resources, we may process:

- Resource search, request, reservation, approval, rejection, issue, return, cancellation, and overdue records
- Book or equipment name, request date, issue date, due date, return date, booking status, and availability status
- The identity and role of the student requesting a resource and the staff member handling the request
- Usage history for books, lab equipment, posted resources, and related administrative actions

### 2.5 Notifications and technical information

When users access the Platform, we may process:

- Notifications about requests, approvals, rejections, due dates, overdue items, classroom activity, and newly posted resources
- Notification read/unread status and delivery preferences
- Device, browser, application version, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, date/time of access, pages or features used, error logs, and security logs

The Platform does not require precise GPS location unless a future feature clearly requests it and provides a separate notice where required.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use personal data to:

- Create, authenticate, maintain, and secure authorised user accounts
- Assign access based on institutional role, department, classroom, and permissions
- Enable users to browse, upload, download, share, request, reserve, approve, issue, return, and track resources
- Display relevant authorship and resource information, such as the faculty member or student who posted material and the date it was posted
- Process library and lab booking workflows, availability, due dates, returns, and overdue management
- Send service-related notifications and reminders, including a due-date reminder that may be generated before an active booking’s return deadline
- Provide dashboards, institutional oversight, aggregate usage analytics, audit trails, and system administration
- Investigate misuse, enforce these Terms and Conditions, prevent fraud, protect Platform security, and comply with legal or institutional obligations
- Maintain, troubleshoot, test, and improve the Platform

We do not sell personal data. We do not use student or staff personal data for behavioural advertising.

## 4. Legal Basis and Institutional Authority

Where applicable, the Platform processes personal data because it is necessary to provide the institutional service, administer campus resources, maintain security, meet legal or regulatory obligations, or pursue legitimate educational and operational interests of the Institution. Where consent is required for an optional feature, such as an optional profile photograph or non-essential communication, we will seek it or provide an appropriate choice.

Users should contact the Institution’s designated privacy contact if they need information about the specific legal basis, institutional policy, or local-law requirements that apply to their account.

## 5. Who Can Access Information

Access is role-based and limited to what is needed for Platform functions.

Student/Faculty/Library Staff/Lab Staff

We may also disclose information when required by law, a lawful government request, a court order, or to protect the rights, safety, property, security, and academic integrity of the Institution, users, or others.

## 6. Visibility of Content and Profiles

The Platform is intended for an authorised campus community; it is not a public social network. Resources and associated metadata may be visible to authorised users according to role, department, classroom membership, and the resource’s access settings.

For example, a note posted to a classroom can be visible to eligible members of that classroom, and it may show the contributor’s name, subject, and date posted. Library and lab staff can view user details necessary to process requests and returns. Administrators may access records required for account management, audit, and institutional oversight.

Do not assume that material uploaded to the Platform is private merely because it is academic. Share only material you are authorised to share.

## 7. Retention

We retain data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes in this Policy, including account administration, academic-resource management, records retention, security, dispute resolution, and legal compliance.

- Active account and profile data are generally retained while the account remains active.
- Booking, issue, return, and audit records may be retained after an account is disabled where needed for inventory accountability, overdue resolution, institutional records, or legal obligations.
- Uploaded academic content may remain available until it is removed by an authorised uploader, faculty member, administrator, or according to institutional retention rules.
- Security logs may be retained for a limited period appropriate to security, troubleshooting, and compliance needs.

The Institution should define and publish specific retention schedules before production use.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal data. These may include role-based access controls, password protection, encryption in transit, secure credential handling, access logging, backups, and restricted administrative access.

No system is completely secure. Users must protect their credentials, use a unique and strong password where permitted, sign out of shared devices, and promptly report suspected unauthorised access or security concerns.

## 9. Your Choices and Rights

Subject to applicable law and institutional requirements, users may request to:

- Access or correct inaccurate personal information
- Update permitted profile information
- Request deletion or restriction of information where applicable
- Object to or request review of certain processing where applicable
- Obtain information about how their personal data is used
- Withdraw consent for optional processing, where processing relies on consent
- Raise a concern with the Institution or an appropriate data-protection authority

Some requests may be limited where data must be retained for academic administration, inventory accountability, legal compliance, security, or the rights of other users. To submit a request, contact the Institution using the contact details in Section 13.

## 10. Children and Student Users

The Platform is intended for authorised members of an educational institution. If the Institution permits use by individuals below the age at which independent consent is valid under applicable law, the Institution is responsible for obtaining any required parent, guardian, or authorised representative consent and for configuring access appropriately.

## 11. Third-Party Services and Links

The Platform may rely on third-party infrastructure or may link to external educational resources. Those third parties have their own privacy practices. This Policy does not govern third-party websites, files, or services that the Institution does not control. Users should review the relevant third-party privacy notice before providing information to an external service.

## 12. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the Platform, institutional practices, law, or security requirements. We will post the revised version with an updated “Last updated” date and, where appropriate, provide additional notice through the Platform or institutional email. Continued use after an effective update is subject to the updated Policy, to the extent permitted by law.
